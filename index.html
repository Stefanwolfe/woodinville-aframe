The Residence

A rare A-frame on over an acre, where vaulted timber ceilings and walls of glass meet a private Woodinville setting.

Built around a dramatic stone fireplace that rises through the heart of the home, this thoughtfully updated A-frame offers modern living wrapped in the warmth of natural materials. Mature landscaping, a fire pit, a sports court, and covered outdoor rooms extend the living across the full 1.10-acre property.
